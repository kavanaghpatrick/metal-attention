## Learnings

- Q6_K format: 210-byte super-blocks, 256 values. ql[128] + qh[64] + scales[16] + d[2]. Dequant: (ql_lo | qh_bits<<4) & 0x3F - 32) * scale * d
- Existing Rust dequant at gpu_weight_store.rs lines 205-262 is the reference implementation
- matvec_q8_0.metal is the exact template for Q6_K kernel (256 threads, 8 SG, simd_sum)
- multi_token_matvec_q4_0.metal is the template for batched Q6_K variant
- Page alignment: GGUF general.alignment=32, but Metal needs 4096. Page-offset wrapper is the right approach.
- forward_prompt() is already parameterized for GQA (q_dim, kv_dim separate). Mistral-7B may work already.
- lm_head dispatch chain: Q8_0 > F32 > Q4_0. Q6_K slot needs to go at top priority.
- GpuKVCache only has reset(), needs truncate() for speculative decode rollback
- SmolLM vocab (49K) != Mistral vocab (32K) -- speculative decode tokenizer mismatch is a known blocker
- Foreman analysis from 4 expert agents (PM, TECH, UX, QA) provided extensive research -- incorporated into spec
- cargo clean -p metal-attention-kernels needed when adding new .metal shader files
